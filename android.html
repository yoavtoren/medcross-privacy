PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 10, 2026 (Android edition)

This Privacy Policy explains how Yoav Toren ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the MedCross application ("App"). The most current version of this policy is also published at:

https://yoavtoren.github.io/medcross-privacy/android.html

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

SUMMARY

  • We do not operate our own backend server. Your game progress, coins, library, and email-account credentials all stay on your device.
  • The online Friends features and leaderboards run on Apple's iCloud and are not part of the Android app, so the Android app publishes no profile to other players. See section 12.
  • We display ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's Android advertising ID. You can reset or delete it, or opt out of ads personalization, in Android Settings.
  • We offer email-and-password sign-up or guest mode. Email accounts are device-local; the password is hashed (PBKDF2-SHA256, 100 000 iterations, unique salt) and never transmitted.
  • We do not sell personal information. We use PostHog for product analytics and error reports under an opaque player ID (never your email). See section 5.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Stored only on your device

  • Game progress, completed levels, and unlocked medical terms.
  • Coin balance and a local record of in-app purchases.
  • Daily login streak counter and notification preferences.
  • Friends list, friend nicknames you choose, 6-digit player codes you have added, and your blocklist.
  • Achievements, XP, and gameplay statistics.
  • Promo code redemption history.
  • Onboarding completion state.
  • Profile display name, avatar selection, and nationality flag (if set).

1.2 Sign-in options

The Android app offers email-and-password accounts and guest mode. Sign in with Apple is not available on Android.

1.3 Email + Password (Device-Local)

If you sign up with an email and password, the email address and a cryptographically-hashed form of your password (PBKDF2-SHA256, unique 128-bit salt, 100 000 iterations) are stored only on your device. The plaintext password is never stored, never transmitted, and never leaves your device. Because we do not operate a backend, an email account created on one device cannot be used to sign in on a different device, and we cannot reset a forgotten password.

1.4 Guest Mode

If you continue as a guest, we generate an anonymous local identifier. No identifying information is associated with your data.

1.5 Information processed by advertising and purchase partners

When you view ads or make purchases inside the App, our third-party providers (Google AdMob and Google Play) may collect information directly from your device under their own privacy policies. See section 5.

1.6 Diagnostic information

If the App crashes, Android may collect crash diagnostics that Google makes available to us in aggregated form via the Google Play Console. The App also sends product-usage events and error reports to PostHog under an opaque player ID, never your email (section 5).

1.7 Online friend features

The friend-request, gifting, and leaderboard features run on Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) and are not available in the Android app. The Android app does not publish a game profile to other players.

1.8 Feedback you send us

If you use the in-app feedback / review form, the text you write (and the basic app-version context attached to it) is transmitted to us and stored in a private issue tracker so we can read and act on it. Please do not include personal or sensitive information in feedback.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use the limited information described above only to:
• Operate the App and track your game progress, coins, achievements, and preferences.
• Schedule local daily reminder notifications on your device if you opt in.
• Record, restore, and validate purchases through Google Play.
• Display interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob to keep the App free.
• Enforce your local blocklist and respond to user reports.
• Meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We use PostHog (product analytics and error reports) to understand how the App is used and to fix crashes; it receives an opaque player ID, never your email.

3. PAYMENT DATA

All purchases within the App are processed by Google Play's billing system. Google collects and processes your payment information. We receive only a purchase token. We do not collect, process, or store any payment card data, Google Pay credentials, or banking information.

4. ADVERTISING

We display ads through Google AdMob, a service operated by Google LLC. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), approximate location inferred from your IP address, device characteristics, and ad interaction data.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, at any time in Android Settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we present a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested, letting you accept or decline the collection and use of your data for advertising. You can revisit this choice at any time from Profile → Ad Privacy Choices. If you decline, only non-personalized ads are served.

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The App uses the following third-party services. We do not control their data practices once information reaches them.

  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases. https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob — advertising. https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • PostHog — product analytics and error reports under an opaque player ID. https://posthog.com/privacy
  • GitHub (private issue tracker) — stores in-app feedback you choose to send (section 1.8). https://docs.github.com/privacy
  • Notifee (local notifications) — schedules daily reminders on your device. No notification tokens or device identifiers leave your device.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY & AGE REQUIREMENT

MedCross is intended for users aged 13 and older. During onboarding, you must confirm you are at least 13 years of age before continuing.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. MedCross does not include in-app chat, direct messaging, or other features that allow children to communicate with strangers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used the App, please contact us at yoavtoren@gmail.com.

7. YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of access — request a copy of personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate information directly in-app.
  • Right to erasure — delete your account via Profile → Settings → Delete Account, or by deleting the App.
  • Right to restriction of processing — sign out, opt out of ads personalization, or delete the App.
  • Right to data portability — export your data from Profile, or request a copy.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Right not to be subject to automated decision-making — we do not use your information for such decisions.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Legal basis for processing (GDPR Article 6): performance of contract (operating the App), consent (notifications, personalized ads, age confirmation), legitimate interests (non-personalized ads, abuse prevention), and legal obligation.

To exercise any of these rights, email yoavtoren@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. CALIFORNIA RESIDENTS (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising, and to non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

We do not sell personal information in the traditional sense. However, the use of Google AdMob for personalized advertising may be considered "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising under the CCPA. To opt out, turn off ads personalization or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings.

To exercise any California right, email yoavtoren@gmail.com.

9. OTHER COUNTRIES, GLOBAL PRIVACY RIGHTS

The App is distributed worldwide. Wherever you live, we extend the same baseline privacy rights to you: access, correction, deletion, objection, and withdrawal of consent. This includes, without limitation, rights under Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, Australia's Privacy Act 1988, Israel's Privacy Protection Law, Japan's APPI, South Korea's PIPA, and any other national, state, or provincial privacy law that applies to you.

Because virtually all MedCross data is stored only on your own device and never reaches any server of ours, you can exercise most of these rights instantly and directly: edit your profile in-app, opt out of ads personalization in Android Settings, or erase everything via Profile → Settings → Delete Account (or by deleting the App). For anything else, email yoavtoren@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

10. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

Your MedCross data remains on your device until you delete the App, sign out, delete your account, or reset your data. MedCross data is excluded from Android cloud backups. Third-party providers retain data under their own retention policies.

You can permanently delete your account at any time via Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This clears all locally-stored MedCross data.

To request deletion without the App (for example after uninstalling it), email yoavtoren@gmail.com with the subject "MedCross deletion request" and your player code if you have it (Profile, under your name). We delete the usage analytics and error reports linked to your player ID and any feedback you sent, and confirm within 30 days. Everything else MedCross keeps exists only on your device and is removed when you delete the App.

11. DATA SECURITY

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information: Android app-private storage, TLS-only network communications, no plaintext HTTP, no backend server of our own, no stored plaintext passwords (email-account passwords are PBKDF2-SHA256 hashed with a unique 128-bit salt and 100 000 iterations on-device before storage), and no stored payment information (handled by Google Play).

No security measure is perfect. If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law (GDPR Article 33: within 72 hours where applicable).

12. FRIENDS FEATURE

The Friends feature and both leaderboards run on Apple's iCloud and are not available in the Android app. The Android app has no contact discovery, no address-book access, no chat, and no messaging.

13. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

We do not operate servers of our own. Our third-party providers (Google, PostHog, and GitHub) may process information in the United States and other countries and maintain appropriate data-transfer safeguards.

14. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. For material changes, we will notify users through the App or by other reasonable means before the change takes effect.
