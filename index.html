MedCross Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

MedCross ("we", "us", or "our") is operated by Yoav Toren. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use the MedCross app.

Summary

MedCross is designed to be privacy-friendly. We do not operate a backend server — your game progress, coins, friends list, and library all stay on your device. We do show ads via Google AdMob, and we offer optional sign-in via Apple. The sections below detail each data flow.

Information You Provide

Account information (optional)

You can use MedCross without an account, sign in with your Apple ID, or create an account with email and password.

  • Sign In with Apple: Apple manages this flow. Apple may share your Apple user ID, name, and email address with us depending on your preferences. We store this information only on your device.
  • Email / password sign-up: Your email and password are stored only on your device. We do not transmit them, and we do not operate a backend that holds them.

Profile preferences

You may optionally set a display name, an avatar, and a nationality flag for use in Trivia matches. All of these are stored only on your device.

Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device. None of it is synced to a server we operate, and none of it is shared between your devices:

  • Game progress, completed levels, and unlocked terms
  • Coin balance and purchase receipts
  • Daily login streak and notification preferences
  • Friends list, friend nicknames, and 6-digit player codes
  • Achievements, XP, and statistics
  • Promo code redemption history
  • Onboarding and tracking-permission state

Third-Party Services

Apple App Store & StoreKit

In-app purchases (coin packs, World Pass, Pro subscription) are processed by Apple via StoreKit. Apple handles all payment information — we do not see or store your payment details. We only receive a receipt confirming the purchase. See apple.com/legal/privacy.

Sign In with Apple

If you choose to sign in with Apple, Apple handles authentication and may share your name and email with us according to your selection.

Google AdMob

We display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising IDs (IDFA), approximate location (inferred from IP), and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads. If you grant permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt, AdMob may use these identifiers to deliver personalized ads; if you deny permission, AdMob will serve non-personalized ads. See policies.google.com/privacy.

Notifee (local notifications)

We use the Notifee library to schedule daily reminder notifications on your device. These notifications are scheduled and delivered locally — no notification tokens are transmitted to any server, and we do not use push notifications.

App Tracking Transparency

MedCross presents the App Tracking Transparency prompt the first time you complete onboarding. Your response controls whether Google AdMob may access your IDFA for personalized ads. You can change this choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Friends Feature

The Friends feature uses 6-digit player codes that you manually share with people you know. There is no in-app contact discovery, no address-book access, no search, and no chat. Friend additions are stored only on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not operate a cloud backend or store your data on our servers.
  • We do not collect or transmit your real-world location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • We do not include chat, messaging, or other user-generated content features.
  • We do not sell or rent any personal information.

Children's Privacy

MedCross is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because game data is stored only on your device, deleting the app removes all data associated with your use of MedCross.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
