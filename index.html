MedCross Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

MedCross ("we", "us", or "our") is operated by Yoav Toren. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use the MedCross app.

Information We Collect

MedCross collects minimal data necessary to operate the app:

  • Purchase History: When you make an in-app purchase, transaction data is processed by Apple and RevenueCat to validate and fulfill your purchase.
  • Device Identifier: A device-level identifier is used by RevenueCat solely to validate in-app purchases and prevent fraud.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not use data for tracking or targeted advertising.

Data Storage

Your game progress and coin balance are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

We use RevenueCat to process in-app purchases. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children's Privacy

MedCross is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
